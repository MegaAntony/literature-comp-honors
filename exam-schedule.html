1. Solving Trigonometric Equations (STE)
  • Factoring quadratic-form trigonometric expressions.
  • Solving trig equations by squaring both sides (always check for extraneous solutions).
  • Solving trig equations involving multiple angles (e.g., sin(2θ), cos(3θ)).

2. Simplify, Rewrite & Evaluate (SRE)
  • Sum & Difference Identities: sin(α ± β), cos(α ± β), tan(α ± β).
  • Product-to-Sum and Sum-to-Product Identities.
  • Double-Angle and Half-Angle Identities.
  • Power-Reducing Identities.

Course 276259 • Science

D. Biology I Honors — Dr. Irwin

Room 371

🚨 Part 1 (MCQ): Fri, Oct 30 • Part 2 (SA): Mon, Nov 2

Formative Lab Packet Check: Wed/Thu, Oct 28–29

1. Enzymes (Unit 3A)
  • Biological catalysts that lower activation energy.
  • Active site, enzyme-substrate complex, induced fit.
  • Denaturation factors: extreme temperature, pH shifts, salinity.
  • Competitive vs. non-competitive inhibition.
2. Cellular Respiration (Unit 3B)

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 36–38 ATP

  • Glycolysis: Cytoplasm, anaerobic, 2 net ATP + 2 NADH + 2 pyruvate.
  • Krebs Cycle: Mitochondrial matrix, releases CO₂, makes 2 ATP, NADH, FADH₂.
  • ETC & Chemiosmosis: Inner mitochondrial cristae, O₂ is final electron acceptor (forms H₂O), ~32–34 ATP via ATP Synthase.
3. Photosynthesis (Unit 3C)

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

  • Light-Dependent Reactions: Thylakoid membrane; splits H₂O (releases O₂), generates ATP & NADPH.
  • Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent): Stroma; fixes CO₂ via RuBisCO into G3P / glucose.
  • Algae Beads Lab: CO₂ indicator color changes in light vs. dark.
